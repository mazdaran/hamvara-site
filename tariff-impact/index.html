03

Evidence and control

AUDIT-READY DESIGN

Versioned rules

Every rate, exclusion, cap and stacking relationship carries effective dates, source status and a rule-set version. Draft rules never appear as confirmed law.

Accounting boundary

Duty and fees are presented separately from material cost. Posting treatment is a company accounting-policy decision; the tool does not create journal entries.

Five-year evidence package

Exports include inputs, assumptions, applied rules, rejected rules, calculation timestamp and source status to support customs recordkeeping workflows.

Human approval

Unverified HTS codes, unresolved stacking, missing metal-origin fields and estimated shipment fees are clearly flagged for broker or controller review.

Regulatory scope and limitations

This tool produces planning estimates only. It is not customs, legal, tax, accounting or audit advice, and it does not make a binding classification or origin determination. Importers remain responsible for reasonable care, entry data and supporting records. Confirm HTS classifications, Chapter 99 provisions, exclusions, origin claims and duty treatment with a licensed U.S. customs broker or qualified adviser.

Reference framework: HTS data published by USITC; CBP entry and recordkeeping requirements including 19 CFR Part 163; USTR Section 301 notices; Presidential proclamations and Federal Register notices. Rule records must be source-verified before production publication.
