What does SKU Bridge check?

SKU and price are required. Duplicate SKUs are checked without case differences; hyphens and underscores are preserved. Numeric fields must be non-negative. GTIN barcodes need 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits with a valid check digit. Empty optional fields are allowed. Currency codes are checked for three uppercase letters only. This does not confirm barcode ownership, currency validity or compatibility with a particular ERP.

An ambiguous number such as 1,234 needs an explicit source number format. Edits use a decimal point without thousands separators: 1234.50. Blank lines are counted and skipped; malformed CSV files are rejected instead of silently losing columns. Formula-like text receives a protective apostrophe in CSV output.

For Excel, select the worksheet and header row. Formula cells and merged cells are not imported; export values to CSV first. Excel may already have rounded long numeric identifiers — store original codes as Text. Excel import needs internet access to load the SheetJS reader.
