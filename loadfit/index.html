Loading calculator questions

What does the free calculator show?

Loaded quantity, cartons and pallets, volume and payload utilization, empty capacity, freight per unit, risk score and a top-view plan.

Can carton dimensions be rotated?

Yes. LoadFit checks unique length, width and height orientations when rotation is allowed and compares them with a buffered safety scenario.

Does it replace a load engineer?

No. Actual securing, axle distribution, reefer airflow, dangerous-goods segregation and packaging strength require carrier and qualified professional verification.
