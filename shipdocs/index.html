COMMON QUESTIONS

Designed for global routes, not one corridor

Can I enter every country?

Yes. Route fields accept any country, city, port, airport or logistics place instead of limiting the user to a short preset list.

Does AI send a document automatically?

No. Planned AI features populate an editable draft. Email is a Pro action and must happen only after explicit human approval.

Is the packing list legally sufficient?

Not necessarily. Required documents and fields vary by goods, route and transaction. ShipDocs is a consistency and drafting tool, not regulatory approval.
