# Customer handover checklist

- Confirm licensed customer, sites, users, roles, and support contacts.
- Deliver the identified release artifact and checksum.
- Retain approved Factory Acceptance, Go-Live, and Hypercare records.
- Confirm opening balances and open transactions with signed reconciliation totals.
- Train key users for backup, receipt, warehouse, planning, production, costing, and reporting workflows.
- Explain support SLA, incident severity, escalation, maintenance windows, and release policy.
- Provide backup, restore, rollback, and data-export instructions.
- Record customer acceptance, known non-blocking limitations, and the first service-review date.
