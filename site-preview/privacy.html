Privacy policy

What this review website does, and what must be finalized for the paid service.

Review copy — not the final sales policy

These texts are prepared for owner review. Business details, retention periods and commercial terms must be finalized before real payments are enabled.

This review website

The preview does not collect uploaded product files or accept payments. Its language links and pricing controls work in the browser. It does not add analytics, advertising trackers or a contact-submission backend. The hosting provider may process routine request logs.

Contact and external links

Email, telephone, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Instagram buttons open the selected service. Information you choose to provide there is handled under that service's own privacy terms. Do not send passwords, card details or confidential product files in an initial inquiry.

Planned SKU Bridge processing

The tested launch implementation reads product files in the browser for mapping and review. Approved rows are sent to the export service to verify access and produce a paid output. A blanket promise that all processing always stays in the browser would therefore be inaccurate.

Accounts and billing

The planned online service needs account identifiers and subscription/payment status to manage access. If Paddle is activated, payment information is handled through Paddle's checkout under its applicable notices. Hosting, subprocessors and retention periods for the online service still need to be finalized.

Choices and requests

Contact info@hamvara.com with privacy or deletion questions. The final notice must explain applicable rights, request handling, retention, international transfers and the appropriate legal bases for the actual service. The operator's full details must be confirmed.

Different Hamvara tools

The links in the catalog open existing tools with different features and data flows. This draft does not assert identical processing or storage behavior across every product.
