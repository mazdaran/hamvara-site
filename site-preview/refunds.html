Cancellation & refunds

A clear route for subscription cancellation and refund requests.

Review copy — not the final sales policy

These texts are prepared for owner review. Business details, retention periods and commercial terms must be finalized before real payments are enabled.

Current status

No real payment is accepted on this preview website. This draft describes the intended support route once live subscriptions are available.

Requesting help

Contact info@hamvara.com with the purchase email, order reference and a short description of the issue. Do not send a full card number, password or security code. Hamvara can help investigate product-access or export problems.

Purchases processed by Paddle

For a future Paddle purchase, the applicable Paddle buyer and refund terms govern the payment process alongside any mandatory rights and additional rights Hamvara grants. Refund requests can use the receipt's support route or Paddle.net. Eligibility is assessed under the applicable policy; no unconditional refund guarantee is stated here.

Subscription cancellation

For Paddle subscriptions, cancellation can be requested through its customer portal or support route. It prevents future renewal charges at the end of the billing period; cancellation does not itself refund an earlier charge.

Rights and final policy

Mandatory consumer rights are not excluded. Any additional Hamvara refund window, trial arrangement or commercial guarantee must be chosen and stated clearly before paid subscriptions open.
