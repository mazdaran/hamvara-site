    Use MRP for manufacturing operations or SKU Bridge for preparing product files. Watch the introduction and read the process below it.

    The tour uses sample data. Product buttons open the current public applications, which may differ from the launch preview. Begin with sample data.

MRP getting started

  1. Confirm your version and workspace

    Open MRP and confirm whether you are using a demo, SaaS workspace or portable installation. Use a demo or a backup copy for practice.

  2. Prepare your master data

    Create or review product codes, units of measure and warehouses. Avoid changing existing codes without checking their movement history.

  3. Check stock and BOMs

    Enter or import opening balances using the template supplied in your version. Review BOM quantities and units, then compare physical stock with the records.

  4. Work through one order

    Enter a small sample order, review material requirements and shortages, then follow the production workflow available in your version.

  5. Review before posting

    Check quantities, warehouse selections and approvals before posting. Review finished goods, movements and delivery records. Export or back up your work using the controls provided in your version.

SKU Bridge getting started

  1. Prepare a copy

    Keep the original file. Start with the downloadable CSV sample and make sure product codes and barcodes are treated as text.

  2. Open and map

    Open the public beta, load a supported file and review the available column mappings. The public beta may differ from the launch workflow shown in the video.

  3. Inspect exceptions

    Review duplicate product codes, missing values, units and decimal separators. Do not assume a duplicate is safe to delete.

  4. Review the output

    Use the review and export controls available in your version. Check leading zeros, quantities and required fields in the exported file.

  5. Test the destination

    Compare the export with the destination ERP import template. Try a small batch in a test workspace before importing live data.
