Supplier claim questions

Which discrepancies are checked?

Short quantity, wrong SKU, unit-price conflicts, invoice-versus-PO differences, receipt gaps, damage evidence and delivery-delay signals.

Does the AI invent missing values?

No. Missing or uncertain facts must remain marked as unknown and reduce claim strength. Every finding should point to document evidence.

Can I send the result immediately?

Review it first. Contracts, Incoterms, carrier rules and local law may set different notice periods or evidence requirements.
